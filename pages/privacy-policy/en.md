# Privacy Policy

**Peach — Ear Training**

*Effective date: August 8, 2026*

[Deutsch](de)

## Overview

Peach is an ear training app that helps you develop your musical hearing through pitch discrimination, pitch matching, and rhythm exercises. Your privacy is important — Peach is designed to work entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting any personal data.

## Data Collection

Peach does not collect any personal data. No account creation is required, and no personal information is requested at any point.

## Data Storage

All training data — including pitch discrimination results, pitch matching results, and rhythm exercise results — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

## Third-Party Services

Peach does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. There are no telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking services of any kind.

The app uses a small number of open-source libraries: [MIDIKit](https://github.com/orchetect/MIDIKit) for MIDI controller input and [swift-async-algorithms](https://github.com/apple/swift-async-algorithms), together with the libraries those two depend on. All of them operate entirely on-device, and none collects or transmits any data.

## Data Sharing

Peach does not share any data with third parties. There is no server-side component, no cloud sync, and no network communication.

## Data Export

You may export your training data as a CSV file through the app's settings. This export is initiated and controlled entirely by you. The exported file remains on your device until you choose to share it.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
